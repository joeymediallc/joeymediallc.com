About Joey Media LLC

Joey Media LLC is an independent media and software studio based in Georgia. We work at the intersection of product and content — building mobile apps on one side, and creating the short-form video that helps products and brands reach an audience on the other.

The studio is owner-operated, which means the person planning a project is the same person building and shipping it. We take on a focused slate of work so each app and each piece of content gets real attention.
